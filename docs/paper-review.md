Use this page to connect OrcaPen's current geometry and simulation guides with
the literature cited for interface and surface-loss modeling.

## Cited literature

The [Surface Loss and Participation Studies bibliography](papers/surface-loss-participation.md)
is the canonical list of the four papers cited in this documentation for
surface-interface and participation context. Each entry states its reading
focus. Compare that scope with your model's geometry, materials, and extraction
method; cited parameters are not automatic process defaults.

## Reading sources and model choices

| Material in a guide | What to show the reader |
| --- | --- |
| Direct quotation or source excerpt | Cite the source and a locator such as its page, section, or table. |
| Reorganized explanation | Cite the source or sources that support the explanation. |
| Original derivation | State the source inputs and starting assumptions, then show the steps. |
| Engineering or model choice | State the choice and its assumptions; distinguish them from source-reported facts. |
